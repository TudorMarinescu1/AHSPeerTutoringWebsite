About Agoura High School Peer Tutoring

Our mission is to make academic support welcoming, dependable, and student-centered. Peer Tutoring helps students build confidence, strengthen study habits, and get extra guidance from trained peers in a supportive environment.

How the program helps students

Tutoring sessions focus on homework help, test preparation, essay support, and subject review. Students can ask questions, practice problem-solving strategies, and leave with a clearer plan for success in class.

Peer Tutoring Board

Board names and roles are shared only after school approval. Until then, program updates are coordinated through the tutoring room staff.
